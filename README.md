# Shopping Behavior Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview



This project analyzes **3,900 customer records** to understand shopping behavior and sales performance. An interactive dashboard was created in **Microsoft Excel** to summarize key performance indicators (KPIs) and explore sales across product categories, customer age groups, seasons, locations, subscription status, and shipping preferences.

The goal is to turn customer transaction data into clear business insights that can support sales analysis and business decision-making.

## Business Questions



The dashboard was designed to answer the following business questions:



1. **Which product categories contribute the most to sales?**  
Compare the sales contribution of Accessories, Clothing, Footwear, and Outerwear.
2. **Which customer age group contributes the most to sales?**  
Compare the Adult, Senior, and Teenager segments.
3. **How are sales distributed across seasons?**  
Examine the sales contribution of Fall, Spring, Summer, and Winter.
4. **Which locations have the highest sales?**  
Identify the five leading locations based on sales.
5. **What proportion of customers are subscribers and non-subscribers?**  
Understand the split between customers with and without a subscription.
6. **Which shipping preferences are most commonly selected?**  
Compare customer counts across Store Pickup, Standard, Next Day Air, Free Shipping, Express, and 2-Day Shipping.



## Key Findings

* **Clothing** is the largest sales category, contributing **44.73%** of sales. Accessories contribute **31.83%**, Footwear **15.49%**, and Outerwear **7.95%**.
* **Seniors** represent the largest age-group share in the chart at **39.97%**, followed by Adults at **37.51%** and Teenagers at **22.51%**.
* Sales are distributed relatively evenly across seasons. **Fall** has the highest share at **25.75%**, followed by Winter (**25.14%**), Spring (**25.18%**), and Summer (**23.93%**).
* **Montana** is the top-selling location among the five locations displayed, with sales of **5,784**. Illinois, California, Idaho, and Nevada are the other locations shown.
* **27%** of customers are subscribers, while **73%** are non-subscribers.
* **Free Shipping** has the highest customer count among the shipping preferences shown (**675**). 2-Day Shipping has the lowest count (**627**) among the displayed options.



## Dashboard Features

* KPI cards for total sales, total customers, average order value, subscriber count, and average review rating.
* Charts for category sales, age-group sales, seasonal sales, top-selling locations, subscription status, and shipping preferences.
* Interactive Excel slicers for:

  * Gender
  * Subscription Status
  * Category
  * Location

The slicers allow users to filter the dashboard and explore different customer segments.

## Business Recommendations

* Review inventory and merchandising priorities for the Clothing category, which contributes the largest share of sales.
* Consider age-group preferences when planning customer-focused campaigns.
* Use the relatively balanced seasonal sales distribution when planning sales activities throughout the year.
* Investigate the leading locations to understand their sales performance.
* Explore opportunities to convert non-subscribers into subscribers and evaluate subscription performance over time.
* Review shipping preferences alongside delivery costs and customer feedback before changing shipping options.



## Project Outcome

The project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used to analyze customer transaction data, define business questions, create KPI-driven visualizations, and communicate findings through an interactive dashboard.
